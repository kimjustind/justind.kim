About

Systems analyst by day. Tinkerer the rest of the time.

As a tinkerer, Justin usually pokes at stuff until he understands it. He has been learning Godot on and off, nominally to make games, but mostly he just plays them.

Justin grew up on games and still plays them. He especially enjoys story-driven stuff like JRPGs and visual novels, boomer-shooters, and indie games that try something different. Hollow Knight is his current favorite video game.

Recently picked up baking and guitar, partly to cut down on screen time.
