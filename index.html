Justin Kim

I spend a lot of time building things — side projects, game prototypes in Godot, scripts that solve some problem I couldn't stop thinking about. Making stuff is how I think.

Games are a big part of my life, both as a player and a maker. My tastes run wide: story-driven games, boomer shooters, and the kind of indie titles that do something you've never quite seen before. Hollow Knight is my favorite game, and I think about it more than I probably should.

I'm driven by curiosity — I'll fall deep into a subject, pull it apart, and come out the other side with a better understanding of how things work. That's true whether it's a game mechanic, a new tool, or something I had no business learning at 2am.
